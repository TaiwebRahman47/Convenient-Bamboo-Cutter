## 📝 Executive Summary

Bamboo is an eco-friendly, versatile, and structurally strong material widely used in rural Bangladesh for construction, agriculture (e.g., betel leaf stakes), fishing traps, and handicrafts. However, traditional manual bamboo processing is labor-intensive, time-consuming, and prone to severe physical strain and injuries from thorns/splinters. High-end automated motorized bamboo cutters exist, but their cost and dependency on electricity make them impractical for small artisans and farmers.

The **Convenient Bamboo Cutter** addresses this problem by introducing a manually operated, pulley-driven mechanism capable of generating high impact/ramming force without electrical power. Designed with interchangeable cutter blades, it offers a safe, multi-functional, highly affordable, and accessible solution to process bamboo efficiently into custom shapes.

## 💡 Project Overview & Background

During the initial phase of product concept generation, eight distinct product ideas were evaluated (including coconut cutters, stair-cleaning devices, and pesticide sprayers). The **Convenient Bamboo Cutter** was selected due to its direct socio-economic impact on rural communities and small-scale industries.

### Target Audience & Market Needs
Based on a field survey conducted across multiple regions in Bangladesh (Jhenaidah, Brahmanbaria, Cumilla, and Barishal) with 32 regular bamboo users:
- **88%** had never used a bamboo cutting machine before.
- **41%** identified physical pain and **25%** identified time consumption as their main issues with traditional methods.
- **62%** preferred a cost range within **5,000 – 10,000 BDT**.
- **59%** expressed direct interest in purchasing a cheaper, pulley-operated manual cutter.

## ⭐ Key Features

- **Pulley-Assisted Force Amplification:** Amplifies manual pulling input into high kinetic impact through a plunger/rammer setup.
- **Multi-Shape Interchangeable Blades:** Easily adjustable blade assembly to split bamboo into various dimensions and strip profiles.
- **Off-Grid Operation:** 100% mechanical (no electricity or motor required), minimizing operational expenses.
- **Ergonomic & Safe:** Features non-slip rubber feet, secure clamping slots, and integrated safety clearance to eliminate manual splinters and recoil injuries.
- **High Affordability & Durability:** Manufactured using readily available, high-strength raw materials such as Mild Steel and High-Speed Steel (HSS).

## 🔬 System Architecture & Design Analysis

### 1. Quality Function Deployment (QFD)
Using the House of Quality (HOQ), customer expectations were translated directly into technical specifications.

* **Top Ranked Technical Requirements:**
  1. Component Design ($23\%$)
  2. Pulley System Setup ($17\%$)
  3. Cutter Blade Sharpness ($13\%$)
  4. Manufacturing Cost Optimization ($11\%$)
  5. Main Body Structural Strength ($9\%$)

### 2. Functional Decomposition
The system was broken down structurally and functionally using standard design frameworks:
- **Black Box Model:** Converts operator mechanical energy and raw bamboo into kinetic ramming energy to yield sliced bamboo.
- **Component Hierarchy:** Partitioned into three primary sub-assemblies:
  - **Main Frame:** Base frame, table legs, bamboo support, storage shelf, supporting rubber feet.
  - **Cutter Assembly:** Cutter holder, replaceable HSS cutter blades.
  - **Moving Parts:** Pulley system, nylon rope, composite rammer, guide bearings.
- **FAST Diagram:** Outlines logical pathways answering *How*, *Why*, and *When* each mechanical function takes place during operation.

### 3. Material & Manufacturing Process Selection
Quantitative selection was executed using the **Digital Logic Method** combined with performance indices ($\gamma = \sum \alpha \cdot \beta$):

| Sub-Assembly Component | Selected Material | Key Selection Criteria | Selected Manufacturing Method | Selected Joining Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frame & Table Legs** | Mild Steel | Cost, Availability, Structural Strength | Welding / Machining | Arc Welding |
| **Cutter Blade** | High-Speed Steel (HSS) | Hardness, Wear Resistance, Toughness | Forging, Machining & Grinding | TIG Welding |
| **Cutter Holder** | Cast Iron | Hardness, Mechanical Strength, Low Cost | Casting & Machining | Nut-Bolt (Temporary) |
| **Rammer (Impact Head)** | Steel Shell + Concrete Core | High Mass/Impact Force, Low Raw Material Cost | Casting & Fabrication | Riveted to Bearings |
| **Supporting Feet** | Natural/Synthetic Rubber | High Friction/Grip, Dampening | Molded / Machined | Epoxy Adhesive |
| **Bamboo Support** | Bamboo / Hardwood | Light Weight, Availability, Low Cost | Machining | TIG / Fasteners |

### 4. Finite Element Analysis (FEA / ANSYS)
Static structural analysis was conducted on critical load-bearing and impact components to ensure structural integrity and infinite life cycle operation under expected loading conditions:
- **Cutter Blade & Holder:** Evaluated for total deformation, equivalent (von-Mises) stress, fatigue life, and factor of safety.
- **Rammer Assembly & Pulley Holder:** Analyzed for transient force propagation during collision/ramming.
- **Bearings:** Verified against high compressive and rolling shear loads.

## 📊 Cost & Financial Analysis

A comprehensive cost structure and break-even analysis was performed to validate commercial viability:

- **Estimated Target Selling Price:** ~5,000 – 8,000 BDT (matching public survey preferences).
- **Direct Manufacturing Costs:** Raw materials (MS, HSS, Cast Iron, Rubber), machining operations, and assembly labor.
- **Overhead & Non-Manufacturing Costs:** Administrative overhead, marketing, and equipment depreciation.
- **Sensitivity Analysis:** Analyzed key cost drivers (Direct Material, Labor, Demand Sensitivity) to ensure profit margins under market fluctuations.
